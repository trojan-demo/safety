Comprehensive Safety Features & Standards

Safety is the bedrock of Trojan Construction Group. We enforce rigid site protocols, daily supervisor audits matching international standards, and proactive hazard prevention.

Every active morning begins with a mandatory 42-point checklist inspection covering housekeeping, scaffolding tags, heavy machinery, fire safety, and electrical ELCBs.

Working at Height & Scaffold Tags

Rigid Green Tag (Safe) and Red Tag (Incomplete) system. 1-in-4 ladder angles, full toe boards, edge protection, and 100% tied harnesses for any work above 1.8 meters.
